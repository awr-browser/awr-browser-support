AWR Browser

Support

A free browser for Hong Kong and Macao, intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Effective: 19 November 2026

Add bookmarks

Open a webpage, choose Add bookmark, confirm its name and save. You can also add an address from Home.

QR sharing

Select 1–5 bookmarks and export ordinary QR codes containing addresses and names. Anyone with the images can read them. The recipient scans or selects the images, previews the addresses and confirms import. Old encrypted sharing codes are unsupported; ask the sender to export new codes.

Local file sharing

Under Settings, choose Local file sharing. Select up to 20 files or videos and tap Start sharing. On another device on the same Wi-Fi, open the displayed URL or scan its code in a browser. Only the selected files are available. Keep the app and sharing panel open. Stop sharing, close the panel, move the app to the background, or disconnect or change Wi-Fi to end the session. Wi-Fi sharing supports private IPv4 and usable IPv6 addresses. Networks providing only IPv6 link-local addresses cannot start a share, and some routers block connections between devices. Sharing does not upload files to a server. Selected files are copied temporarily on your device; stopping retains the selection for an explicit restart. Closing the sharing panel or restarting the app deletes those temporary copies without deleting your originals.

Filtering and exceptions

Manage the global switch and website exceptions under Settings > Ad blocking. Add an exception if a website does not display correctly. Some advanced syntax is unsupported.

Video playback

Play local files, direct video URLs and extractable ordinary web videos. DRM, blob/MSE and some cross-origin videos must remain in the original webpage. Native network playback is subject to system security and format restrictions; HTTP links may not play, so HTTPS is recommended, and you can return to the original webpage for restricted content.

Report a problem

Email your app version, iOS version, steps and expected result. Do not send private bookmarks or your complete browsing history.
